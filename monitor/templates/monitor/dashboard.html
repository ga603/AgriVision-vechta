Welcome to AgriVision Münsterland
How to use this AI Prototype:
  • Locate Your Farm: Allow GPS location access to instantly jump to where you are standing, or pan the map manually.
  • Draw Boundaries: Use the polygon tool (the pentagon icon on the left of the map) to click the exact corners of your physical field.
  • Analyze: Select your date range in the sidebar and click "Run AI Analysis". The Copernicus Sentinel-2 satellite will scan the area.
  • Review: The AI will overlay red grids on areas experiencing crop stress. A PDF report will automatically download for your records.
